# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (6 Nov 2026): archived before the sealed test

This release is archived with a DOI **before** the sealed test sample (5-18 Nov 2026)
is drawn on or after 19 Nov 2026. It fixes, with a public date:

* the model: `models/detector`, hash 81062b5eb365, trained 29 Sep-24 Oct 2026;
* the hand labels used for every decision so far (`labels/`), and the calibration
  table on the map (`models/score_bands.json`);
* the integrity-only baseline for the Phase 2 gate (share of aircraft with degraded NIC,
  s = 0.30 primary, 0.25 secondary) and the six sealed-test questions, including the
  0.1 label threshold (docs/evaluation_protocol.md).

Nothing in it may be changed for the sealed test; the next release reports its result.

**Correctness (Phase 0).** Point format points/2 with air data (airspeed, heading, roll,
wind); full re-ingest of 15 Sep-4 Nov 2026 (51 days) and a re-ingest check (identical
rows and features); decode errors and changed region boxes mark a day for re-run;
input failures withhold a day, a control alarm publishes it as "needs review"; hourly
gap check; every published result regenerated by `gnssguard benchmark` with hashes.

**Independent evidence (Phase 1).** Public event catalog (19 events, 5 with ground
measurements): 14 of 15 checkable events found (95% interval 70-99%). Hand review of
calm-region flags: about 0.1% false alarms among judged cells. Calibration set (150
cells): reviewer share by score band shown on the map. Blind relabel: 39 of 39.

**Signals and baselines (Phase 2).** Model against the integrity-only baseline on
development sets: 86% against 76% of interference, 56 of 80 spoofing cells against 0.
Air-data and position-spoofing signatures explored and documented; none added.

**External validation (Phase 3).** Ground check 1 (six EUREF stations around
Kaliningrad, 26 Oct-4 Nov 2026): no agreement with the map beyond a shifted-days
control, as stated before the data; `gnssguard ground-fetch` and `ground-check`.

**Product (Phase 4).** Incident pages: one page per catalogued event with the map, a
timeline, the evidence, an ordinary-day comparison and how to reproduce it
(`gnssguard incidents`, site: Incidents).
