Data files

Everything on the map is a plain file you can download or read from code. There is no key and no rate limit: the files are static. Their format is versioned as gnssguard-public/1; a change that breaks it will get a new version number. Additions keep the version: since 4 November 2026 the label column and the map files can also hold the three _sparse labels below (map codes 6-8, listed in each file's label_codes), and the summary gives sparse_flagged_cells. Days published earlier show those cells as low_coverage unless they were redone.

Where the files are

FileWhat it holds
data/index.jsonEvery processed day, newest first, with each region's status, and the latest published day.
data/<day>/summary.jsonPer region: status, quality-check reasons, aircraft seen, cells judged, affected shares. Model versions.
data/<day>/<region>.csv.gzOne row per cell and 15-minute window with any aircraft (gzip-compressed CSV).
data/<day>/<region>.jsonThe same for the map: per cell, one value per window.
data/<day>/incidents.jsonGroups of jammed cells, with an affected area and, for good fits, a likely jammer circle.

Days are UTC dates (2026-10-26). Regions: baltic, black_sea, east_med, punjab, central_europe (calm control).

Columns of the cell table

ColumnMeaning
cell_lat, cell_lonSouth-west corner of the 1° cell.
window_startStart of the 15-minute window, UTC.
usable_aircraftAircraft whose GPS quality could be checked. Empty when it was 1 or 2.
p_jamming, p_spoofingModel scores, calibrated against the system's own training labels: of cells scored 0.8, about 8 in 10 carried those labels in testing. Not validated probabilities of real interference: almost every cell scores below 0.1 or above 0.9, and on hand-labeled cells a score of 0.9 or more was interference 97% of the time, below 0.1 7% of the time (a third of the time over the Black Sea). Each file also carries reviewed_score_bands: for scores below 0.1, 0.1-0.9 and 0.9 or more, the share of hand-reviewed cells a reviewer called interference (10%, 60% and 92% on the calibration set of 15-21 Sep 2026). The label uses the uncalibrated score at 0.5; both are stated in every summary and map file (score_meaning, label_threshold).
labelnormal, jamming, spoofing, both with 3 or more aircraft checked. With 1 or 2: jamming_sparse, spoofing_sparse or both_sparse when the model flags the cell (right 47 of 47 times on a hand-labelled day; not counted in the shares), otherwise low_coverage. Cells without aircraft are not listed: no row means no data, not normal.

Reading it from code

import json, urllib.request
import pandas as pd

base = "https://sahilprg.github.io/gnss-interference/data"
day = json.load(urllib.request.urlopen(f"{base}/index.json"))["latest"]
cells = pd.read_csv(f"{base}/{day}/baltic.csv.gz")
jammed = cells[cells["label"].isin(["jamming", "both"])]

Rules for use

  • Not for navigation, flight planning or any operational decision.
  • A jammer circle is a statistical estimate of where one ground transmitter would best explain the affected aircraft. It is not evidence of who operated it.
  • The files are derived from adsb.lol data and are shared under the Open Database License 1.0: credit "adsb.lol and the gnssguard project" and share derived databases under the same licence.
